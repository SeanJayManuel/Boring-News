## Rules

1. Every transaction is recorded here within a week.
2. Donations pay only for running costs: hosting, the domain, and API usage. Nobody is paid from donations.
3. Donations never buy influence over sources, labels, ranking or writing.
4. If donations ever exceed costs, the surplus and what happens to it are stated here.
