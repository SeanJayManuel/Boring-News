How we choose

No one picks the stories by hand. A story makes the edition when outlets across the political spectrum covered it, and each one gets a score:

Breadth is the share of left, center and right outlets that covered it. Coverage counts outlets on a log scale that stops at . Diversity counts kinds of outlet: wire, national, regional and international. A story also needs at least outlets.

Official sources, such as Congress, the Federal Register and agency releases, are listed separately whether or not the news covered them.

Summaries are written from the outlets' reporting. A fact is stated plainly only when at least two outlets agree on it. Otherwise it is attributed.
